GST Refund under Inverted Duty Structure

Refund of ITC accumulated - CGST RULE 89(5)

\[ Refund = \left( \text{Net ITC} \times \frac{\text{Inverted Turnover}}{\text{Adjusted Total Turnover}} \right) - \left( \text{Tax Payable} \times \frac{\text{Net ITC}}{\text{Total ITC}} \right) \]

"Net ITC" shall mean input tax credit availed on Inputs oods during the relevant period.

GST Refund under Export (Zero-Rated) under LUT/Bond

Refund of ITC paid on Exports - CGST RULE 89(4)

\[ \text{Refund Amount} = \frac{\text{Net ITC} \times \text{Zero-Rated Turnover}}{\text{Adjusted Total Turnover}} \]

"Net ITC" means input tax credit availed on inputs goods and input services during the relevant period.

🚀 Quick Guide - Start Here

What this calculator does

This tool estimates GST refund for:

  • Refund under inverted duty structure
  • Refund of input tax credit for exports under LUT or bond

Enter month-wise data. The system applies the rule formulas and shows refund for each month and for the full year.

Useful for workings, planning and reconciliation before filing on the portal.

What you should keep ready

Keep these figures for every month:

  • Zero rated turnover (actual invoice value)
  • Zero rated turnover (FOB value)
  • Non-inverted taxable turnover
  • Inverted taxable turnover
  • Tax payable on inverted supply
  • ITC on inputs
  • ITC on input services

Once available, the calculation becomes plug and play.

How to enter data – 3 easy ways

  • Manual: Type values directly in the cells.
  • Paste: Click a column header to paste 12 months together.
  • Excel: Upload the Excel template to auto-populate instantly.

Important logic you should know

For zero rated turnover, actual value is compared with FOB value. Normally the lower figure is taken.

If one value is zero, the higher figure is considered. This prevents distortion where only one number exists.
